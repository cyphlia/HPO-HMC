\subsection{Empirical Validation: Joint Hamiltonian Conservation}
\label{subsec:energy_conservation}

A central theoretical guarantee of Hamiltonian dynamics is the conservation of total energy $H(\theta, \lambda, p_\theta, p_\lambda) = T(p_\theta, p_\lambda) + V(\theta, \lambda)$. In Figure~\ref{fig:energy_conservation_live}, we present an empirical validation of energy conservation measured during a live Method C training run on the joint HamiltonianNN system.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{energy_conservation_live.png}
  \caption{Empirical validation of joint Hamiltonian conservation during live Method C training. (Left) Energy drift across 40 leapfrog sub-steps without gradient clipping ($+2.35\%$) vs.\ with clipping ($+263.4\%$). (Right) Potential energy across 60 HMC proposals with Metropolis acceptance status.}
  \label{fig:energy_conservation_live}
\end{figure}

Without gradient clipping, the unclipped leapfrog integrator exhibits near-exact energy preservation with a relative drift of only $+2.35\%$ over 40 sub-steps. This confirms the physical validity of the augmented Hamiltonian system. When gradient clipping is applied ($\text{grad\_clip}=10.0$), energy conservation is intentionally relaxed to prevent momentum explosion near steep gradients.

\subsubsection{Statistical Significance Tests}
\label{subsec:stats}

Following Dem\v{s}ar~\cite{demsar2006}, we apply the Friedman test across all 11 datasets and 5 methods to obtain a global $p$-value. Nemenyi post-hoc pairwise tests with critical difference (CD) at significance level $\alpha=0.05$ determine which pairs of methods are statistically distinguishable. A CD diagram is provided in Figure~\ref{fig:cd_diagram}.

The Friedman test yields $p = 7.92\times10^{-4}$ across the 11 datasets and 5 methods, and the Nemenyi critical difference at $\alpha=0.05$ is $\mathrm{CD} = 1.84$. Since the rank gap between Optuna~TPE (rank~$1.36$) and Method~C (rank~$3.09$) is $1.73$, which is \emph{less} than the CD, the two methods are \emph{not} statistically distinguishable at this significance level. Optuna~TPE is significantly better only than Method~A (rank difference $2.73 > 1.84$) and Random Search (rank difference $2.27 > 1.84$).

\subsection{The Optimizer's Curse: Validation-Overfitting Gap}
\label{subsec:optimizers_curse}

A crucial empirical phenomenon discovered during clinical evaluations is the \emph{Optimizer's Curse} (or Winner's Curse of model selection). When an optimization algorithm evaluates many candidate configurations against a small, noisy validation set, selecting the maximum score systematically overfits to validation noise.

Table~\ref{tab:optimizers_curse} quantifies the validation-minus-test AUROC gap on the Diabetes benchmark across 5 seeds.

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
